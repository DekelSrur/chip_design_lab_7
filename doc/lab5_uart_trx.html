Lab 5 UART TRX

The lab 4 transmitter grows into a TX/RX controller. SW[15] picks the direction, the center button must be held for a second to latch the switches, and in RX mode a 16-byte message {R…,C…,V…} is received, parsed and shown on the 7-segment display.

Notes

  • The PC-to-FPGA line on the Nexys A7 is pin C4, named uart_txd_in in the master XDC (the name is from the USB chip's side). Connect it to rx.
  • The synchronizer is the same two-flop pattern as btn_ff in lab 4 sync; you can add rx there and feed rx_s straight in.
  • Keep CLKS_PER_BIT, the debounce time and the 1 s hold time as parameters, as in lab 4, so the testbench can shrink them (the spec asks for a 100 ns button press in simulation).
  • The spec gives two meanings for T3 in TX mode: transmitted rows (section 5) and transmitted data bytes (section 8). The diagram follows section 8; check with your instructor which one is expected.
